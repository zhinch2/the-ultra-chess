## What you need

All you need to play UltraChess is a standard chess board along with chess pieces.

## What you need to know

* You need sufficient background knowledge of how chess works and how the pieces move as that is not explained in this guide.
* You also need to have carefully read these instructins before attempting to play UltraChess.
