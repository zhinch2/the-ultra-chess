# Rooks

Rooks are lvl 3 pieces with a value of 5. Just like knights and bishops, they combine with pawns to create a piece of higher value, which in this case would be a [queen](/queens-ultraqueens) (lvl 3 + lvl 1 = lvl 4). Rooks can not combine with itself or any other piece that is not a pawn.
