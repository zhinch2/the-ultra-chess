# Knights and Bishops

Knights and Bishops are both lvl 2 pieces with a value of 3, which is why they are in the same category. Both Knights and Bishops can combine with a pawn or 2, but they cannot combine with each other. When they combine with a pawn, they become a lvl 3 piece (a [rook](/rooks)).
