# Queens and Ultra Queens

As you and I know from regular chess, the queen is the most valuable piece in the game. Or should I say, was. The queen is now the second most valuable piece, below the Ultra Queen. The ultra queen is a lvl 4 piece + a lvl 1 piece, with its new value being 15. The only way that a queen can combine with another piece to create an ultra queen is if the original queen (one that has not been created) is in the same square that a pawn would promote to.
